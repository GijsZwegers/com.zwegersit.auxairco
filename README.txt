Bring your AUX Freedom split-unit air conditioner into Homey. Turn it on or off, switch between cooling, heating, drying and fan modes, set the target temperature and fan speed, and see the current room temperature, all straight from Homey and your Flows, no separate app needed.

This app talks to the same cloud service as the AC Freedom and AUX Home apps, so your air conditioner stays fully in sync no matter which app you use to control it.
