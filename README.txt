Control your AUX air conditioner from Homey and your Flows: switch it on or off, choose a mode, set the temperature and fan speed, and read the room temperature.

Before installing
Your air conditioner must already be set up and working in AC Freedom or AUX Home. You need an AUX account with an email address and password. An AUX brand name alone does not guarantee compatibility. Tested with one AUX Freedom 5.0 kW split unit; other models and older cloud accounts are not guaranteed to work. Support for the older cloud service is experimental.

Using Homey and the AUX phone app together
Use a separate AUX guest account for Homey. Create a second account with its own email address and password, then share your air conditioner with that account using AC Freedom or AUX Home. Accept the invitation if required and check that the air conditioner is visible to the guest account. Sign into Homey with the guest account and keep using your main account in the phone app. Using the same account in both can disconnect Homey's session. This is an AUX account, not a guest user in Homey.

Connection and login
An internet connection and the AUX cloud service are required; local control is not supported. If AUX rejects your login or locks the account, Homey stops automatic login attempts and shows a message. For a lockout or lockout warning, wait at least ten minutes without trying to log in, then use the device's repair option in Homey to sign in again. Your device and Flows are preserved.
